Yeshua HaMashiach

  • 4-6 B.C. - Born in Beit-Lechem (Bethlehem), Judea, during the reign of King Herod. His birth fulfills various prophecies of the Tanakh (Hebrew Bible)
  • Circa 4 B.C. - Escapes to Egypt with Miriam (Mary) and Yosef (Joseph) to avoid the massacre of infants ordered by Herod in Beit-Lechem.
  • After 4 B.C. - Returns to Netzaret (Nazareth), in Galilee, where he grows up and learns carpentry with Yosef.
  • At age 12 - Found teaching in the Beit HaMikdash (Temple) in Jerusalem, astonishing the scholars with his understanding of the Scriptures.
  • At age 30 - Baptized by Yochanan HaMatbil (John the Baptist) in the Jordan River, marking the beginning of his public ministry.
  • 30-33 A.D. - Travels through Judea and Galilee, preaching, healing the sick, performing miracles, and gathering followers. Shares parables like the Shomroni Tov (Good Samaritan) and the Prodigal Son.
  • Circa 33 A.D. - Enters Jerusalem triumphantly during Pesach (Passover), an event celebrated as "Palm Sunday."
  • 33 A.D. - Betrayed by Yehuda Ish Keriot (Judas Iscariot), arrested, tried, and crucified under the governance of Pontius Pilatus (Pontius Pilate). Dies on the cross to atone for humanity’s sins.
  • Three days later - Resurrects from the dead, appearing to his disciples and hundreds of others over 40 days.
  • Ascension - Ascends to heaven, promising to return one day.
