Yeshua HaMaschiach: The Son of God

Yeshua HaMashiach is God incarnate who came into the world and dwelt among humans, spreading goodness and love wherever He went. His miracles drew everyone's attention.

*Image for illustrative purposes only.

Biography

  • He was born in Beit Lechem, Yehudá.
  • From a young age, He stood out in His knowledge of the Torah.
  • He grew up peacefully until He began His ministry at the age of 30.
  • His mother was named Miriyam, and His father was named Yossef.
  • He was conceived by the very Spirit of God, who placed Him in Miriyam's womb.

Ministry
