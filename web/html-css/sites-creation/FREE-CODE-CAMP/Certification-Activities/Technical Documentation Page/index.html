Introduction

Christianity originated in the 1st century CE in the Roman province of Judaea as a sect of Judaism, with Jesus of Nazareth as its central figure.

It emerged from the teachings of Jesus, who was a Jewish rabbi and preacher, and the subsequent actions and beliefs of his followers.

The spread of Christianity, initially focused on Jewish communities, expanded to include Gentiles, and eventually led to its separation from Judaism.

Christianity believes in Jesus of Nazareth to be the Messiah the jews were waiting for. Christianity also believes that Jesus is the Son of God and also God.

According to Christianity, God is a triune being: God The Father, God The Son and God The Holy Spirit (or The Holy Spirit of God).

Christianity has the Holy Bible as the proper Word of God, which is the base of the christians's faith.
